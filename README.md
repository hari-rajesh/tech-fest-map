#Tech-Fest-Map

📍 College Symposium Map
A digital map designed to help visitors navigate our college during the symposium.

✨ Features:
🗺️ Venue & Event Locations – Easily find where each event is happening.
🚗 Parking Areas – Designated spots for hassle-free parking.
🍽️ Food Stalls – Locate nearby food stalls and refreshment areas.
🚻 Restrooms – Quick access to restroom locations.
🏨 Hostel & Accommodation – Directions for guest stays.
📍 Other Key Locations – Any additional important places around the campus.

This interactive map is useful for students and visitors from other colleges, ensuring they can navigate the symposium without confusion. 🚀
